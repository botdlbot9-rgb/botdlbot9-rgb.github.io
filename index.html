🧭 Your First Steps Inside the App

When you open the CRM for the first time, the screen can feel busy. Start with just three things and the rest will make sense quickly.

Step one: add a contact. Click the contacts area and create a new record with a name and email address. This is the foundation of everything else in the system.

Step two: create a deal. Attach a deal to that contact and give it a value. Assign it to the first stage of your pipeline, usually called something like "New" or "Appointment scheduled."

Step three: set a follow-up task. Add a reminder for a few days from now. When it appears, you will know exactly who to call and why. This one habit keeps a pipeline healthy.

💡 Tips for a Healthy Pipeline

  • Keep stage names short and honest. If a deal is not really a deal yet, leave it in an early stage until a real conversation happens.
  • Log every call and email the same day. Details fade fast, and future you will thank present you.
  • Use tasks instead of memory. A short daily list of follow-ups is the single biggest difference between a busy week and a productive one.
  • Review your pipeline once a week. Deals that have not moved in a month usually need a nudge or a polite goodbye.
  • Tag contacts by source so you can tell which channel actually brings in customers, not just clicks.
